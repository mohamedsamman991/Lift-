WHO WE ARE

What is LIFT?

LIFT stands for Literacy is the Foundation of Tomorrow. We believe that learning can open doors, build confidence, and create opportunities.

Our goal is to encourage a world where every child has the opportunity to learn, grow, and discover their potential.
